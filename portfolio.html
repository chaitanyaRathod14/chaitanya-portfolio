01 · About
A bit of background
Add
profile.jpg

Trying to explain my work to family usually ends with, "I build websites that not only look good but actually do something cool."

Hey there — I'm Chaitanya Bhaurao Rathod, a 3rd-year Information Technology student who's happiest turning ideas into working products. Most days that means MERN stack development, but I'm just as comfortable soldering a sensor as I am debugging an API.

Between coding sprints, you'll find me building full-stack applications, fine-tuning embedded systems, or leading a team through a hackathon. Agro Shield — a farm protection system I built and later filed a patent for — is a good example of what happens when a real-world problem meets a soldering iron and too much curiosity.

I'm also active in tech communities and event management, where I get to practice the parts of engineering that don't show up in a code editor: collaboration, coordination, and getting a room full of people to agree on a plan.

Outside of that, I'm usually on a football field, tinkering with a DIY hardware project, or planning the next event. I believe in staying curious and letting work and play blur together a little.
